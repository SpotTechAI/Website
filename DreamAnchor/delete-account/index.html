How to delete your account and data.

DreamAnchor is a self-help app, not medical treatment. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure anything.

How to delete your account

  1. Email hello@spottechai.com with the subject "Delete my DreamAnchor account".
  2. Send it from the email you saved your account with, or include that email. We may ask you to confirm.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Never saved your account with an email? Save it in the app first, then email us from that address. Otherwise we can't find your anonymous account.

Want a copy first? Export your drift logs as a CSV file in the app. You can also delete single logs without deleting your account.

What happens

  • Deleted: your account, email and all synced data (logs, progress, settings, worlds, timeline and encrypted writing), plus your subscription record at RevenueCat.
  • Kept: nothing, except payment records that Apple or Google keep under their own policies.
  • Timing: within 30 days. Backup copies expire within a further 30 days.
  • On your phone: your photo, added sounds, lock and reminders are removed when you delete the app.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account doesn't stop billing. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
