Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

  • Last updated 10 November 2026

DreamAnchor is a self-help app, not medical treatment. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure anything. In a crisis, call your local emergency number or visit findahelpline.com.

In short

  • You don't need to give us your name or email.
  • Your writing is encrypted on your phone before it's stored.
  • No ads, no trackers, and we never sell your data.
  • You can export your logs and delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

DreamAnchor is made by SpotTechAI, which is responsible for your data (the "controller" under GDPR and the "Data Fiduciary" under India's DPDP Act 2023). Contact us at hello@spottechai.com.

2. What we collect

The app creates an anonymous account when you first open it. We only get your email if you choose to save your account (email plus a 6-digit code), so you can restore your data on a new phone or after reinstalling.

Your writing is encrypted on your phone before it's stored, and protected by your account login and access rules. The key is kept with your account so signing in restores everything, which means this isn't end-to-end encryption. We don't read your writing, use it for ads or run AI on it.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. There's no push server and we collect no device tokens.

Analytics: none today. We may later add anonymous, privacy-friendly analytics (such as TelemetryDeck) without advertising identifiers, and will update this policy first.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

3. What stays on your phone

Your profile photo, sounds you add, your Face ID or passcode lock (handled by iOS — the app never receives biometric data) and your help-line country (taken from your phone's region setting — we never ask for your location). These are never uploaded, so they don't move to a new phone.

We never show ads, use ad trackers, track you across apps or websites, sell or share your data for advertising, or use AI or automated decisions on your personal content.

4. Who we share with

  • Supabase — our database and sign-in provider.
  • Apple and Google — sell and process subscriptions. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat — we use it to manage subscription status. It receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. For people in the EEA and UK we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. We only disclose data otherwise if the law requires it.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

We use your data to provide the app you asked for (contract); with your explicit consent for anything about your wellbeing you choose to log or write, which you can withdraw by deleting it; to keep the service secure (legitimate interests); and where the law requires (legal obligation).

6. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Logs you delete are removed from our server. When you delete your account, we delete it and all synced data — including the RevenueCat record — within 30 days, and backup copies expire within a further 30 days. Apple and Google keep their own payment records.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, your writing is encrypted on your phone, and access rules keep each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure; we'll tell you about a breach where the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, delete and export your data, object to how we use it, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. Export or delete logs in the app, delete your account using these steps, and email hello@spottechai.com for anything else (from your account email, if you saved one). We reply within 30 days.

California: we collect identifiers (account ID and optional email), purchase status, and content you enter, which may include sensitive health information used only to run the app. We don't sell or share personal information. You, or an authorised agent, can ask to know, correct or delete it, and we won't treat you differently for doing so.

India: you can get a summary of your data, correct or erase it, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance with us at hello@spottechai.com. If it isn't resolved, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

DreamAnchor isn't directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA). We don't knowingly collect their data, and if we learn we have, we'll delete it.

10. Changes and contact

We'll update this policy and its date before changing how we handle data, and tell you in the app about important changes. Questions? Email hello@spottechai.com.
