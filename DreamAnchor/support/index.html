Questions

How does the program work?

An 8-week guided program, inspired by published research with original content, helps you notice drifting and take back your time — control, not quit. You also get drift logging, a nightly daydream window, story worlds, focus games, breathing practices, ambient sounds and a weekly review.

Is this therapy?

No. DreamAnchor is a self-help tool, not therapy or medical treatment. If daydreaming is seriously affecting your life, please talk to a professional.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan has a 7-day free trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged; otherwise it becomes a paid yearly subscription.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

How do I restore my purchases?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, then tap Restore purchases on the app's subscription screen.

How do I move to a new phone?

First, save your account with your email on your old phone. Then sign in with that email on the new one. Your profile photo, added sounds, Face ID lock and reminders stay on the old phone.

Can I export my logs?

Yes. You can export your drift logs as a CSV file from inside the app.

How do I delete my data or account?

Delete single logs in the app. To delete your whole account, see Delete your account. Cancel your subscription separately.

How does the Face ID lock work?

The lock is handled by iOS, so the app never sees your face data. If Face ID fails, use your passcode. If it isn't offered, check Settings → Face ID & Passcode.

My reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings → Notifications → DreamAnchor, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. After reinstalling, open the app so it can set them up again.

The voice or sounds aren't playing

Check your phone isn't on silent, turn the volume up, and check any Bluetooth headphones. Sounds you added stay on the phone you added them on.

Is my writing private?

Your writing is encrypted on your phone before it's stored, and protected by your account login and access rules. We don't read it, use it for ads or run AI on it. See the Privacy Policy.
