We turn technology into outcomes, not buzzwords. We find where time, money and opportunity are being lost — then build the solution that wins it back.

SpotTechAI is a technology and artificial-intelligence company focused on solving actual problems. Whether it's an AI assistant, an automated workflow, a data platform or a modern web and mobile experience, we choose the technology that fits the problem — never the other way round.

06 — FAQ

Common questions.

We're new to AI. Where should we start?

Start with a conversation. We'll look at how your organisation works today, identify where AI or automation would make the biggest difference, and recommend a small, high-impact first step.

Do you only work on AI projects?

No. AI is one of our strengths, but we deliver complete technology solutions — websites, apps, automation, data platforms and cloud infrastructure. We pick whatever best solves the problem.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Absolutely. Making technology accessible is core to our mission. We offer flexible engagements that fit different stages and budgets.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

Security and privacy are built in from the start. We follow industry best practices for data handling, access control and encryption, and design solutions around your compliance requirements.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer training, maintenance and ongoing improvement so your solution keeps delivering value long after go-live.
